Switching converters are inside every phone charger, laptop and electric car. In this workshop you will build one yourself.

## What you will do

- Learn how a buck converter steps voltage down by switching, not by burning power as heat.
- Size the inductor and capacitor for a 12 V to 5 V, 2 A design.
- Solder the board, then measure ripple and efficiency with an oscilloscope.

## Who it is for

Second-year students and above. Basic circuit analysis is enough; we provide all components and tools. Places are limited to 24 participants, so register early.

## Bring

A laptop with LTspice installed (free), and your student card.
