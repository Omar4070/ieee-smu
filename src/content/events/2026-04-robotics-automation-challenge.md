Our second Robotics & Automation Challenge brought together twelve teams from four faculties for two days of programming, debugging and very little sleep.

## The task

Each team received a six-axis collaborative robot, a conveyor and a box of mixed parts. The robot had to pick each part from the moving belt, identify it and place it in the right bin. Runs were timed; every wrong placement added a ten-second penalty.

## Results

1. **Team Ampere**: 2 min 41 s, no errors
2. **Team Kirchhoff**: 2 min 58 s, one error
3. **Team Tesla Coil**: 3 min 20 s, no errors

Thank you to our judges from industry and to the faculty for lending the lab equipment.
