Textbook motors and drives look different when there are two hundred of them running a production line. This visit shows how the pieces fit together in a real plant.

## Programme

- **08:30** Bus leaves the faculty
- **10:00** Safety briefing and plant overview by the maintenance team
- **10:45** Robot cells and conveyors: motors, drives and PLCs
- **12:30** Lunch
- **13:30** The plant's medium-voltage substation and power-quality monitoring
- **15:00** Return to campus

## Practical details

Transport is provided. Closed shoes and long trousers are required on the factory floor. Open to all chapter members; priority goes to IAS and IES members.
