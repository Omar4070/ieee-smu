A team of three chapter members placed second at the regional power electronics design contest, out of nineteen university teams.

The brief was to design a 1 kW single-phase inverter for a rooftop solar system and to reach the highest efficiency at half load. The team's design reached 97.1 % and was praised by the jury for its thermal design.

Congratulations to the team, and thanks to the lab staff who opened the power electronics lab on weekends.
